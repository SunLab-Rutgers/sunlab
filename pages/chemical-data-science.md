<section class="course-hero">
  <div>
    <p class="eyebrow">CHEM 487/542</p>
    <h1>Chemical Data Science</h1>
    <p>Chemical Data Science is designed for students and researchers from a broad range of backgrounds. The lectures give a careful explanation of technical details for people who want to understand or develop methods, while the hands-on sessions emphasize practical applications so you can use data science and machine learning in chemistry without needing every derivation on day one.</p>
    <div class="course-balance" aria-label="Course balance between theory and practice">
      <div>
        <span>Lectures</span>
        <strong>Theory and algorithms</strong>
        <p>Concepts, derivations, model assumptions, and technical depth.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="course-balance-bar" aria-hidden="true">
        <span></span>
      </div>
      <div>
        <span>Hands-on</span>
        <strong>Practical applications</strong>
        <p>Coding, chemical datasets, model training, prediction, and interpretation.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

Tenghui Li developed CAFES, an AI agent for targeting strongly correlated molecular, crystalline, and model Hamiltonian systems.

The work is now available on arXiv: [CAFES paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00943).

The software is available on GitHub: [lith0419/CAFES](https://github.com/lith0419/CAFES).

The agent can also streamline dataset generation. The generated dataset is available on Zenodo: [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/23068308).
